Daily three-way reconciliation SOP

Before the vault is closed for the night, three independently maintained totals must agree — physical count, Vault Placement Register and Gold Stock Register. Any mismatch escalates immediately, before the vault is sealed.

1
Physical count

Both joint custodians count every packet in every bin under dual custody, packet-by-packet against the placement register page.

2
Register tally

Vault Placement Register active-status rows are tallied and reconciled against the day's movement events to ensure all placements and releases are logged.

3
CBS Stock tie-out & Seal

The physical count must equal the CBS Gold Stock Register (Opening stock + placements − releases). Both custodians sign the closing entry; the vault is then dual-locked.

How the floor works

Each simulated bid raises the ladder; the bar races toward the gold reserve line. Strike the gavel any time — before the reserve is met the lot goes Unsold and re-queues at the same 90% floor for a second round; once met, it's Sold and the proceeds waterfall runs automatically.

The first auction must be a physical sale in the lending branch's own district. Only if that fails does the lender move to an adjoining district or switch to an online e-auction platform — the route IIFL adopted branch-wide after its 2024 remediation.

≥ 90%of min(30d avg, prev close)
≥ 85%Floor after 2 failed rounds
22KIBJA benchmark purity
Recalc.If purity re-audit differs

Under the RBI 2025/26 baseline the 85% floor only opens up after two consecutive failed auction rounds — the first re-auction must still clear 90%. (Some pre-2026 lender SOPs, e.g. Muthoot's, allowed the drop after a single failed round; the harmonised Directions raise that bar.) Reserve price is also recalculated against reconfirmed purity and net weight if the pre-auction revalidation finds the gold to be lower quality than originally recorded.

Bidder eligibility

PAN + Aadhaar KYC and an Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) are mandatory before bidding. The lender, its group entities and the original valuers are barred — but the original borrower may bid on their own pledged gold. Winning bidders must clear the balance within 5 working days or forfeit the EMD.

Proceeds waterfall

1. Principal 2. Interest 3. Auction / notice charges 4. Surplus → borrower

RBI's "7-day rule": surplus refunded within 7 working days, backed by a ₹5,000/day penalty for late payment. A shortfall becomes unsecured debt — lenders typically route it to either a board-approved write-off or a conversion to an unsecured personal loan ledger, given typical gold-loan ticket sizes.

Not hypothetical: RBI fined IIFL Finance ₹3.10 lakh in May 2026 for exactly this — failing to pay surplus auction proceeds back to borrowers.
