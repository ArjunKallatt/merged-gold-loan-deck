NC-2025-001: LTV Regulatory Breach Repeat Finding (3rd occurrence since Jan 2024)
Vikram Singh (GL-2025-00421) Raised: 18 Oct 2025 CRITICAL CAP Pending Branch
Observation Details
Auditor Finding
During physical gold verification, the current market value of 48g 22K gold was assessed at ₹2,97,600. The outstanding loan balance is ₹4,12,000, resulting in a current LTV of 82.4%. This exceeds the RBI permissible limit of 75%.
Root Cause & Control Weakness (COSO)
Process Failure / Control Ineffective. Branch did not execute the required daily LTV fluctuation monitoring. Margin call was not issued when LTV crossed system limits.
Business Impact
Regulatory Non-Compliance, High Credit Risk (Unsecured portion: ₹1,14,400)
Auditor Recommendation
Immediately issue a margin call notice to the borrower. Recover ₹1,14,400 to bring the outstanding balance down, or obtain additional gold collateral.
